# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes respectful collaboration from people of every background and experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, direct, and constructive.
- Share reproducible examples when reporting migration behavior.
- Keep discussions focused on database safety, adapter behavior, and developer experience.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, spam, or personal attacks are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or related community spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may hide, edit, or remove comments and may block contributors who repeatedly violate this code.
